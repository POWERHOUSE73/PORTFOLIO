About Me

Still early in the journey — and building in the open.

I'm Sachin Bista, a BSc. CSIT student who started this journey in 2079 B.S., drawn to computer science because I genuinely enjoy solving problems and building things. Web development and software engineering are the subjects I connect with most in my coursework, and that's shaped where I want to go — full-stack development, where I can work across both what people see and what makes it run.

I don't have professional experience yet, and I'm not pretending otherwise — right now my focus is learning and building. I've deployed my own portfolio on Vercel and keep iterating on it, and I'm currently building an Online Clothes Thrifting System while picking up full-stack skills, alongside small games and practice projects on the side. I push completed projects and practice work to GitHub, though not on a strict schedule. I haven't done a hackathon or joined a coding club yet, but it's something I want to get into.

Most of what I know outside the classroom comes from self-directed learning — DSA tutorials, CodeWithHarry, and other programming educators, rather than formal certifications. When I hit a problem I don't understand, I break it into smaller parts and experiment until it clicks — that's just how I work through things. Outside of code, I follow cricket, listen to music, watch tech videos, read, travel when I can, and game.

I'm looking for a place — any team, really — where I can learn from people who know more than me, contribute honestly, and keep growing. Every project I've built has taught me something, which is part of why it's hard to pick a favorite; each one moved me forward a little.
